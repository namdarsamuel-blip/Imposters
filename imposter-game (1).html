IMPOSTER
WORD GAME

Hidden words. Cunning lies. One imposter.

HOW TO PLAY
1
SETUP
Choose how many players and how many imposters. You can also toggle whether imposters know each other, show a category hint, and whether the game ends on the first vote.
2
REVEAL YOUR WORD
Pass the phone around. Each player taps their name, sees their secret word privately, then taps Got It and passes it on. Imposters see 🎭 IMPOSTER instead of the word — but may get a vague category hint.
3
GIVE CLUES
Go clockwise around the circle. Each player says one word as a clue related to the secret word. Imposters must bluff — say something that sounds like you know the word without giving yourself away. Everyone can see all the clues as they come in.
4
VOTE
After clues, it's time to vote. Pass the phone around — each player privately votes for who they think the imposter is, or skips. The player with the most votes is eliminated. If majority skip, nobody goes out and you do another round.
5
THE REVEAL
Watch the dramatic reveal — was the voted-out player the imposter or innocent? If caught, the game ends and the secret word is revealed. If innocent, the imposter survives and you play another round!
🎭
IMPOSTER STRATEGY
Listen carefully to everyone's clues before your turn. Say something that could fit the category but isn't too specific. If you survive multiple rounds without being caught, you score more points!
💡
CREW STRATEGY
Listen for clues that are too vague, too weird, or don't quite fit. The imposter often gives broad generic clues. Challenge anyone who seems off — but don't accuse too fast or the real imposter gets away!
